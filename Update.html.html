About the program

Why food matters with sickle cell.

Sickle cell disease changes the shape of red blood cells. That can lead to anemia, pain crises, infections and fatigue.

Many people living with SCD get too little of key nutrients like folate, calcium and B vitamins. Research links better nutrition with healthier hemoglobin levels and fewer hospital visits.

Simpli Nutri-Life pairs short lessons with hands-on cooking, using affordable, culturally familiar food, so the recipes work on a tired day and on a real budget.

  • FeIronPair beans and greens with vitamin C to absorb more.
  • B9FolateHelps your body build new red blood cells.
  • B12Vitamin B12Works with folate to make red blood cells.
  • CaCalciumOften low with SCD. Collard greens help.
  • MgMagnesiumSupports muscles and nerves.
  • KPotassiumSweet potato, plantain and avocado.
  • Ω-3Omega-3Healthy fats from salmon, walnuts and chia.
  • H₂OWaterDehydration can trigger a crisis. Sip all day.

Check with your care teamBring questions about supplements, fluids and iron to your doctor or dietitian. If you get regular blood transfusions, check with them before adding iron-rich foods or supplements.
